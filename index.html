Our story

From an agricultural by-product to a certified premium product line.

Vietnam discards roughly 248,000 tonnes of pineapple fiber every year — the leaf, peel and core that traditionally end up burned at the field edge. Through our joint venture with ECOSOI Vietnam, we recover that fiber, extract its bromelain enzyme, and formulate it into pet care that meets the standards of the US premium-natural segment.

This is circular economy as a supply-chain decision, not a marketing line. Farmers earn a secondary revenue stream, soil pollution is reduced, and our cost basis stays competitive against incumbents like Burt's Bees for Pets and Earthbath.

The science

Six engineering principles behind every RePaws bottle.

Each formulation is traceable from a Vietnamese pineapple field to an FDA-registered facility.

01

Bromelain Enzyme

Recovered from 80–85% of pineapple by-product otherwise discarded. Naturally cleansing, anti-inflammatory, low-irritant.

02

Soapberry Saponin

A traditional Vietnamese cleansing remedy, extracted via proprietary process. Sulfate-free surfactant alternative.

03

Glycerin & Vitamin Complex

Deep moisturization and conditioning for shiny, healthy coats across breeds and skin types.

04

Probiotic Fermentation

Biological process that compresses production cycle by 5× while maintaining 100% pet-safe outputs.

05

Zero Restricted Inputs

No parabens, no sulfates, no synthetic fragrance. Compliant with FDA and EU sensitive-skin standards.

06

Recyclable Primary Pack

PCR bottles and jars labelled with soy-based ink. End-of-life recovery built into the supply chain.

Support

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email support@repaws.co.

Is RePaws safe for cats as well as dogs?

Yes. All three SKUs are tested for cats and dogs, including pets with sensitive skin. Formulations contain no essential oils that are toxic to cats and have passed VNTEST analysis (pH 5.5–7.0, heavy metals ≤ 10 ppm, zero skin irritation, microbial count ≤ 100 CFU/g).

Where is the product manufactured?

RePaws is contract-manufactured at a GMP / ISO 22716 certified OEM facility in Vietnam. Active ingredients (bromelain from pineapple by-product, saponin from soapberry) are supplied by ECOSOI Vietnam, our joint venture partner.

What is the standard delivery window?

US domestic orders ship via Amazon FBA in 2–5 business days. Subscribe & Save shipments dispatch on the 30/60/90-day schedule you select at checkout.

How do I cancel a Subscribe & Save plan?

Pause, change frequency, or cancel anytime from your RePaws account. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum number of shipments.

Is the product tested on animals?

No. RePaws is committed to a cruelty-free policy. Safety is validated through independent laboratory testing and a voluntary 10-household MVP study conducted in the US during pre-launch.

Do you offer wholesale or retail partnerships?

Yes. We are evaluating specialty retail pilots from Year 4 onward (US) and Amazon UK from Year 3. Email wholesale@repaws.co for our partnership deck.
